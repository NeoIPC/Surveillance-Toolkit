# Licensing

The content in this directory is a NeoIPC compilation derived from WHO sources, extended with a few systemic antibiotics that are not in those sources. Full citations and source URLs are in [`README.md`](README.md).

While the repository as a whole is licensed under the [MIT License](https://spdx.org/licenses/MIT.html), we cannot relicense the upstream content under terms that conflict with the licenses chosen by its authors. This list is a **derivative** of the WHO [AWaRe classification of antibiotics, 2021](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/2021-aware-classification) (WHO/MHP/HPS/EML/2021.04) — its Access/Watch/Reserve categories curated into the NeoIPC schema and extended with systemic substances not in the AWaRe list. The AWaRe classification is licensed [CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/igo/), and its **ShareAlike** term requires a derivative to be released under the same licence. The effective license for this directory is therefore the Creative Commons [Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 IGO (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/igo/) license.

The substance names are International Nonproprietary Names — factual identifiers. The ATC codes and the ATC group names are the WHOCC's copyrightable classification, **reproduced verbatim** from the WHO Collaborating Centre for Drug Statistics Methodology [ATC/DDD index](https://www.whocc.no/) (ATC/DDD [copyright and disclaimer](https://atcddd.fhi.no/copyright_disclaimer/)) and **not adapted**, so the ATC no-derivatives terms are not engaged. All are attributed to the WHOCC.

The ATC group **descriptions** were also reproduced verbatim, and are **no longer carried at all** — the column was removed from `NeoIPC-Antibiotic-Groups.csv` because only 17 of 34 groups had one and each restated the group name. Note what this does and does not change: it removes the largest block of reproduced WHOCC prose, but the licence above is unaffected, because the AWaRe category descriptions remain and, more substantially, the classification itself — which substance sits in which group and category — is the upstream editorial work this directory derives from.
