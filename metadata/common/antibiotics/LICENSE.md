The substance names are International Nonproprietary Names — factual identifiers. The ATC codes and the ATC group names are the WHOCC's copyrightable classification, **reproduced verbatim** from the WHO Collaborating Centre for Drug Statistics Methodology [ATC/DDD index](https://www.whocc.no/) (ATC/DDD [copyright and disclaimer](https://atcddd.fhi.no/copyright_disclaimer/)) and **not adapted**, so the ATC no-derivatives terms are not engaged. All are attributed to the WHOCC.

The ATC group **descriptions** were also reproduced verbatim, and are **no longer carried at all** — the column was removed from `NeoIPC-Antibiotic-Groups.csv` because only 17 of 34 groups had one, nothing consumed them, and none had ever been translated. They were not restatements of the group name: they described each group's mechanism of action, spectrum or composition ("*This group comprises tetracycline antibacterials inhibiting the bacterial protein synthesis through binding to …*"), which is precisely why they were the largest block of reproduced WHOCC prose here. Note what this does and does not change: it removes the largest block of reproduced WHOCC prose, but the licence above is unaffected, because the AWaRe category descriptions remain and, more substantially, the classification itself — which substance sits in which group and category — is the upstream editorial work this directory derives from.
